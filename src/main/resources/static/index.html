What is Durak

Durak (Дурак) is a fast card shedding game. Players attack and defend in turn while trying to empty their hands. The last player holding cards is the durak.

  • 2–4 playersIndividual or team play
  • Public or private roomsJoin by table or code
  • Elektronik opponentTwo-player Quick Play

Online play

  1. 1
    Choose a table

    Use Quick Play vs Elektronik, create a room, or join with a six-character code.

  2. 2
    Invite players

    Copy the private link, or leave the room public so it appears under Open tables.

  3. 3
    Attack and defend

    Match ranks when attacking and beat cards with a higher card or a trump when defending.

  4. 4
    Avoid being the durak

    Empty your hand before everyone else. The last player with cards loses.
