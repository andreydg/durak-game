Play Durak online with friends or a bot

Durak (Дурак) is a fast Russian shedding game where everyone tries to get rid of their cards. The last player still holding cards is the durak. Start instantly against a bot, open a public table, or share a private room code with friends—there is nothing to install and no account to create.

How online play works

  1. 1
    Choose a table

    Use Quick play, create a room, or join with a six-character code.

  2. 2
    Invite players

    Copy the private link, or leave the room public so it appears under Open tables.

  3. 3
    Attack and defend

    Match ranks when attacking and beat cards with a higher card or a trump when defending.

  4. 4
    Avoid being the durak

    Empty your hand before everyone else. The last player with cards loses.

Common questions

Durak Online FAQ

Is Durak Online free?

Yes. Play is free in your browser, with no account, download, or installation.

How many people can play?

Durak supports two, three, or four players. In a four-player game, partners sit opposite one another and win or lose as teams.

Can I play by myself?

Yes. Select Quick play to start a private two-player game against a bot immediately.

Can strangers see a private room?

No. Turn off “List my room publicly” and the room stays out of Open tables. Anyone with its code or invite link can still join.
